# Review and record events

## Preview a medicine's schedule

Open **Medicine**, select a medicine, and choose **Calendar**. Scheduled dates are marked in the calendar; select a date
to inspect its entries and move between months. This is a view of MedTimer's schedule, not confirmation that Android
delivered a notification or that a dose was taken.

## Open a reminder from its notification

Tap a reminder notification, including an out-of-stock or expiration date notification, to open **Overview** on the day
the reminder was scheduled for, scrolled to the reminder.
A reminder that was snoozed past midnight still opens on its original day.
If the active filters or the tag filter would hide the reminder, they are cleared.
If the reminder's event was deleted in the meantime, tapping the notification removes it.

## Doses at the same time

On **Overview**, the doses that share one reminder time show together in one card under that time. A single dose has no time header. Taken doses keep their place in the card. Long-press a dose to select every dose of its card. To show every dose in its own card, turn off **Group entries by reminder time in overview** in **Display settings**. This setting does not change notifications. To get one notification for the doses of one time, turn on **Combine same time notifications**.

The time header offers **Confirm all**, which marks every open dose of that time as taken with one tap. Doses that are already taken or skipped keep their state and time. The three-dot button next to it opens the actions for the whole group, for example **Skipped** or **Reschedule reminder**. An action that does not apply to every dose of the group does not show.

## Correct a recorded event

On **Overview**, tap the event's icon to update its status. To edit event details, tap the event's data to open its
editor. Available fields depend on the event; an additional dose can also have its date changed. Check the edited entry
after saving.

## Record an additional dose

On **Overview**, choose **Log additional dose**. Select a medicine, then choose a suggested amount, enter a custom
amount, or select **No amount**. The dialog shows the selected date and the clock time captured when it opened.
Choose **Log at this time** to use that displayed date and time, or choose **Log at another time** and confirm a
different clock time for the selected date. MedTimer confirms when the dose is saved.

**Quick log at this time** shows your three most recently logged additional doses. Tap a row to record that medicine
and amount at the date and time displayed in the dialog. Use the guided flow to change the amount or time.

## Export event history

Open the options menu on **Overview**, **Medicine**, or **Analysis**, choose **Event data**, then select **Export events
as CSV** or **Export events as PDF**. Choose a destination and share the file only with people you intend to
receive it as exports can contain sensitive medication history.
