# Review and record events

This guide is currently available in English only.

## Preview a medicine's schedule

Open **Medicine**, select a medicine, and choose **Calendar**. Scheduled dates are marked in the calendar; select a date to inspect its entries and move between months. This is a view of MedTimer's schedule, not confirmation that Android delivered a notification or that a dose was taken.

## Correct a recorded event

On **Overview**, use the event's **Taken** or **Skipped** controls to update its status. To edit event details, swipe the event to the right and open its editor. Available fields depend on the event; an additional dose can also have its date changed. Check the edited entry after saving.

## Record an additional dose

On **Overview**, choose **Log additional dose**, then select the medicine and enter the amount. If you have an inactive reminder configured with a reusable amount, it can be offered as a preset in the medicine chooser. An additional-dose entry records information in MedTimer; it does not notify a health professional.

## Export event history

Open the options menu on **Overview**, **Medicine**, or **Analysis**, choose **Event data**, then select **Export events as CSV** or **Export events as PDF**. Choose a private destination and share the file only with people you intend to receive it. Exports can contain sensitive medication history; do not attach real exports to support requests or public issues.
