# Customize notification behavior

This guide is currently available in English only. Android notification behavior varies by Android version and device. These options do not guarantee that a notification will be delivered or make a schedule medically appropriate. For missing or late notifications, see [Notifications missing or late](notifications-missing-or-late.md).

## Choose a notification category and sound

A medicine's priority and a reminder's **Notification importance** determine which notification category Android uses. MedTimer's **Notification settings** screens open Android's settings for the relevant category. Change its sound or other available behavior there. Android may expose different controls on different devices.

## Configure snooze and repeated reminders

In **Settings**, **Snooze settings** controls MedTimer's snooze duration. Android also has a system notification-snoozing setting on supported versions; its controls are managed by Android.

**Repeat reminders** controls whether MedTimer repeats a reminder after a delay and how many repetitions it uses. Repeats are not a fix for a missing schedule, a disabled notification category, or a denied permission.

## Use alarm presentation

A medicine can use **High + Alarm** priority. Android may require MedTimer to have access to show full-screen alarms. The app's **Alarm settings** also include ringtone and silent-mode behavior. Review Android's own access and notification settings on your device; selecting alarm priority alone does not guarantee full-screen presentation or delivery.
