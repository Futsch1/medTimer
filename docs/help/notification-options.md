# Customize notification behavior

Help is currently available in English only.

Android notification behavior varies by Android version and device.

These options do not guarantee that a notification will be delivered or make a schedule medically appropriate. For
missing or late notifications, see [Notifications missing or late](notifications-missing-or-late.md).

## Choose a notification category and sound

A medicine's priority or a reminder's **Notification importance** determine which notification category Android uses.
MedTimer's **Notification settings** screens open Android's settings for the relevant category. Change its sound or
other available behavior there. Android may expose different controls on different devices.

## Configure snooze and repeated reminders

In the **Settings** menu, **Snooze settings** controls the snooze duration when the **Snooze** option of a reminder is
selected. When a reminder is snoozed, it is retrigger after a given amount of time.

Additionally, you can set up location based snooze, which will retrigger a notification when the phone is returned to
a specific location. Use this to be reminded again when you return home and have access to your medication.

**Repeat reminders** controls whether MedTimer repeats a reminder after a delay and how many repetitions it uses. When a
reminder is repeated, the notification will appear again after the delay, ringing the sound again. Use this if you want
to be reminded continuously.

## Use alarm presentation

A medicine can use **High + Alarm** priority. Android may require MedTimer to have access to show full-screen alarms.
The app's **Alarm settings** also include ringtone and silent-mode behavior. Review Android's own access and
notification settings on your device; selecting alarm priority alone does not guarantee full-screen presentation or
delivery.
